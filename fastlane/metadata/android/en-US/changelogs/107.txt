xyzterm v3.4.8

- Ubuntu is now optional: no forced download on first launch. Open the terminal immediately, or install Ubuntu when you're ready from the first-run screen or Terminal settings.
- Ubuntu shell setup is smoother: Node.js auto-installs once in the background (no blocking the first prompt), and failed setups log to /.cache/.nodejs.log.
- Terminal startup is more reliable on devices without /sdcard or external storage: missing paths are no longer bound to the sandbox.
- Accessibility improvements: the Ubuntu-not-installed screen is grouped for TalkBack with a heading, and download progress is announced.
