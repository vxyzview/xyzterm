xyzterm 1.0.0

- Real Arch Linux (archm) in a proot container, no root required
- Per-ABI APK: each download contains only your device's rootfs
- Rootfs is unpacked at build time; first launch only copies files locally
- Guided setup: terms, optional permissions, then install progress
- Session-first terminal with searchable settings and themed first shell
- Backup/restore, extra keys, snippets, custom binds and Material You themes
