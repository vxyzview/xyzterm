First stable release of xyzterm as a standalone app.

- Real Ubuntu 24.04 via proot - no root required, per-ABI rootfs
- Full terminal emulator with custom fonts, colors, and extra-keys row
- Multiple named sessions, persisted across restarts
- AMOLED black theme and Material You dynamic color
- Backup and restore of the terminal environment
- Rootfs downloads verified with SHA-256 integrity checks
